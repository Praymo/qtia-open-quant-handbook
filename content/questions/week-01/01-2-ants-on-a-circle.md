## 中文题目

若干只蚂蚁在圆周上等间距分布。每只蚂蚁独立地以相同概率选择顺时针或逆时针方向，并以每分钟绕圆周一圈的速度运动。

当两只蚂蚁相遇时，它们立即同时改变方向，速度大小保持不变。将蚂蚁视为质点。

求一分钟后，下列事件发生的概率：

1. 对于 **9 只蚂蚁**，它们占据的位置集合与初始位置集合相同。
2. 对于 **9 只蚂蚁**，每只蚂蚁都回到各自的初始位置。
3. 对于 **10 只蚂蚁**，每只蚂蚁都回到各自的初始位置。

<details>
<summary>English version</summary>

Several ants are equally spaced on a circle.

Each ant independently chooses clockwise or counterclockwise direction with equal probability and moves at a speed of one full circle per minute.

When two ants meet, both immediately reverse direction while keeping the same speed. Treat the ants as point particles.

Find the probability that after one minute:

1. For **9 ants**, the occupied set of positions is identical to the initial occupied position set.
2. For **9 ants**, every ant returns to its own initial position.
3. For **10 ants**, every ant returns to its own initial position.
