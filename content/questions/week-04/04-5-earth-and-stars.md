## 中文题目

将地球视为不透明的球体，星星视为位于地球外、互不重合的质点，位置可以自由选择。若从地表观测点到某颗星星的视线不穿过地球内部，就认为该星星可见。对于任意正整数 $n$，若要求地球表面的每个观测点都至少能看到 $n$ 颗星星，最少需要多少颗星星？说明理由。

<details>
<summary>English version</summary>

Model Earth as an opaque sphere and stars as distinct point particles outside it, whose positions may be chosen freely. A star is visible from an observation point on Earth's surface if the line of sight to it does not pass through Earth's interior. For any positive integer $n$, what is the minimum number of stars needed so that every observation point on Earth's surface can see at least $n$ stars? Explain your reasoning.

</details>
