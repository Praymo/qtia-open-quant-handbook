## 中文题目

在边长为 $1$ 的无限正方形网格上，判断下列情况下是否总能使变换后的图形不覆盖任何标记点，并说明理由。

1. 标记点为所有格点；图形面积小于 $1$，只允许平移。
2. 每格内部各有一个标记点（位置可不同）；图形面积小于 $1$，只允许平移。
3. **（Optional）** 标记点为所有格点；图形为面积小于 $4/3$ 的凸图形，允许平移与旋转。面积恰为 $4/3$ 时又如何？

<details>
<summary>English version</summary>

Consider an infinite square grid with unit side length. In each case below, determine whether the shape can always be transformed so that it covers no marked points, and explain your reasoning.

1. All lattice points are marked. The shape has area less than $1$, and only translation is allowed.
2. There is one marked point inside each grid cell, with positions possibly differing between cells. The shape has area less than $1$, and only translation is allowed.
3. **(Optional)** All lattice points are marked. The shape is convex with area less than $4/3$, and translation and rotation are allowed. What happens when its area is exactly $4/3$?
