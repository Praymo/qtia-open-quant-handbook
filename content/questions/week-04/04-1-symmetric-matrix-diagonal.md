## 中文题目

设 $n$ 为正整数。从所有关于主对角线对称、且每列恰好包含 $1,\ldots,n$ 各一次的 $n\times n$ 矩阵中等概率选取一个。求主对角线也恰好包含这些数各一次的概率。

<details>
<summary>English version</summary>

Let $n$ be a positive integer. Choose uniformly at random from all $n\times n$ matrices that are symmetric about the main diagonal and whose every column contains each of $1,\ldots,n$ exactly once. What is the probability that the main diagonal also contains each of these numbers exactly once?
