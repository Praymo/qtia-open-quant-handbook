## 中文题目

中秋博饼每轮独立掷六枚骰子。六种点数各出现一次称为“对堂”；恰有四枚掷出 $4$ 称为“四点红”（本题也包括“状元插金花”）。

求下列问题，并说明理由。

1. 六枚骰子均公平时，首次出现对堂或四点红的期望轮数。
2. 五枚骰子公平，另一枚掷出 $4$ 的概率为 $q$。求对堂比四点红更可能先出现的 $q$ 的取值范围。

<details>
<summary>English version</summary>

In the Mid-Autumn dice game, six dice are rolled independently in each round. Rounds are independent. A roll in which each of the six face values appears exactly once is called “Duitang” (对堂). A roll with exactly four dice showing $4$ is called “Four Reds” (四点红); for this problem, this event also includes “Zhuangyuan with Golden Flowers” (状元插金花).

Answer the following questions and explain your reasoning.

1. When all six dice are fair, find the expected number of rounds until either Duitang or Four Reds first appears.
2. Five dice are fair, while the remaining die shows $4$ with probability $q$. Find the range of $q$ for which Duitang is more likely to appear before Four Reds.
