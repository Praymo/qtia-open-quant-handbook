## 中文题目

在一块圆形大月饼内任选一点，过该点沿四个相隔 $45^\circ$ 的方向各切一刀，得到八块。

沿圆周依次把八块交替分给甲、乙。证明两人分得的面积相等。

<details>
<summary>English version</summary>

Choose any point inside a large circular mooncake. Make four straight cuts through this point, with successive cut directions $45^\circ$ apart, producing eight pieces.

Going around the circumference, assign the eight pieces alternately to A and B. Prove that the total areas received by A and B are equal.

</details>
